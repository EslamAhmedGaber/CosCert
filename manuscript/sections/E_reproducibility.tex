\paragraph{Code} \texttt{code/p3}: \texttt{gram.py} (closed-form Gram matrices, double precision), \texttt{lowrank.py}
(certified fixed-$m$ and EWC limits in ball arithmetic), \texttt{certify.py} (certified distribution function of $T_H$),
\texttt{finite\_n.py} (exact finite-sample laws, circulant-embedding sampler, local Whittle estimator),
\texttt{laws.py} (Imhof, Ruben and reference laws). \texttt{code/experiments}: one driver per experiment
(\cref{tab:experiments}); \texttt{code/figures}: one script per figure, all typeset with the preamble of this manuscript;
\texttt{code/manuscript/make\_numbers.py} writes every number and table in this paper from the result files, so that no
number in the text is typed by hand.

\paragraph{Software} Python 3.12 with NumPy, SciPy, mpmath and python-flint (Arb) for all computations; R 4.5 with
CompQuadForm, momentchi2 and survey, and the Python package gx2, for the comparison of \cref{sec:methods}; XeLaTeX
(TeX Live 2025) with Latin Modern fonts for the manuscript and the figures. Run times refer to one Windows machine with
12 logical cores. The versions of every package are recorded in \texttt{ENVIRONMENT.json}, and the SHA-256 checksums of
all result files in \texttt{SHA256SUMS.txt}; the timing runs of \cref{sec:methods} pin the linear-algebra libraries to
one thread.

\paragraph{Numerical safeguards} The exact finite-sample laws diagonalise the positive semi-definite matrix of
\cref{prop:exactn}(b) in double precision; over the \pthree{clip-ncells} design cells, rounding produced at most
\pthree{clip-max} negative eigenvalue per cell (in \pthree{clip-nwith} cells), of magnitude at most \pthree{clip-rel}
of the largest, and these are set to zero. The ARFIMA$(1,d,0)$ autocovariances are summed from their exact series,
truncated where the remainder is below \pthree{arfima-trunc} of $\gamma(0)$.

\paragraph{Status labels} Every number in the result files carries one of the labels \emph{exact}, \emph{certified},
\emph{computed} or \emph{simulated} (\cref{sec:setting}); simulated numbers also carry the number of replications and
the seed.
