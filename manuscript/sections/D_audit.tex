\paragraph{Sources and versions} Every audited value was read from the document listed in \cref{tab:sources}, at the
page and table given there; where only a working paper or preprint was available, the version is stated, and values were
taken from page images when the text layer was unreliable. Each value is stored with its statistic, normalisation, tail,
design and replication count in the machine-readable ledger \texttt{ledger\_critical\_values.csv}
(\pthree{ledger-n-cv} values) and \texttt{ledger\_rejection\_rates.csv} (\pthree{ledger-n-rr} rates).

\paragraph{Conventions frozen before computation} (i) Fractional Brownian motion is of Type~I with $\Var B_H(1)=1$;
$H=\delta+\frac12=(\beta+1)/2$. (ii) The bridge is the pathwise bridge $B_H(r)-rB_H(1)$. (iii) Conversions are those of
\cref{tab:norm}. (iv) A published critical value from $N$ replications is judged against the standard error
$\sqrt{\alpha(1-\alpha)/N}$ of the size, a published rejection frequency $\hat r$ against $\sqrt{\hat r(1-\hat r)/N}$;
the two are never mixed. (v) Verdicts: \emph{accurate within Monte Carlo error} (size deviation within three standard
errors); \emph{inaccurate} only for deviations the design does not explain or above $0.1$\,pp at $5\,\%$;
\emph{unresolved} when the deviation is systematic but its cause cannot be verified from the source. The five classes
A--E of \cref{tab:audit} refine these verdicts, with the precedence rule of \cref{sec:audit}. Class~C (consistent with a
finite design inferred for the source, not with the limit) was added after the Hualde--Iacone pattern at $\delta=-0.49$
had been seen (protocol Amendment~5); class~D (a one-sign pattern within Monte Carlo error) was first assigned by hand
to the McElroy--Politis values at $H>\frac12$ and was replaced by the explicit block rule of \cref{sec:audit}, which
also selects the Hualde--Iacone row $\delta=0$ (protocol Amendment~8).
